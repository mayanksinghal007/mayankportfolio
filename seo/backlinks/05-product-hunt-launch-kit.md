## 2. Product Description (Short & Long)

### Short Description (for search cards):
> Free financial modeling calculator that factors in COGS, shipping, payment gateway fees, and return rates (RTO) to reveal your true break-even ROAS and target CPA for Facebook & Instagram Ads.

### Detailed Description (for main listing body):
> Most media buyers and e-commerce founders look only at Meta Ads Manager ROAS, failing to account for Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), shipping fulfillment, payment processing fees (Razorpay, Stripe), and return/RTO percentages.
>
> **Meta Ads ROAS & Profit Calculator** solves that blindspot. In under 30 seconds, founders and performance marketers can:
> - Model dynamic Average Order Values (AOV) and ad spend budgets
> - Calculate exact Break-Even ROAS and Target CPA
> - Visualize net cash profit after all fulfillment deductions
> - Identify scale ceilings before risking client or store capital
>
> Built with zero tracking cookies, lightning-fast React 19, and full static prerendering. 100% free with no sign-up wall.
