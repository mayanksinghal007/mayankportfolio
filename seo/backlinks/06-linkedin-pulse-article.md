## 1. The Post-iOS Meta Infrastructure

Manual interest targeting is dead. Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns now leverage Meta's global neural networks to find high-intent purchasers far faster than any media buyer can manually configure.

However, ASC campaigns only perform as well as the signal data fed into them. Without server-to-server tracking:

## 2. Knowing Your Exact Financial Thresholds

Before putting budget into any Meta campaign, every media buyer must establish their true **Break-Even ROAS**. 

If your average order value (AOV) is ₹2,500, but COGS, courier shipping, payment gateway fees (2.5%), and COD return-to-origin (RTO) charges consume ₹1,500, your net margin before ad spend is **40%**. 

$$\text{Break-Even ROAS} = \frac{1}{0.40} = \mathbf{2.50x}$$

If your agency delivers a 2.2x ROAS, you are losing money on every shipment!

To help founders model their exact unit economics in real time, I developed the **[Meta Ads ROAS & Profit Calculator](https://mayankaggarwal-one.vercel.app/tools/meta-ads-roas-calculator)**—a free interactive tool that calculates net profit, target CPA, and scaling ceilings instantly.
